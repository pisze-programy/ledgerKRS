Three steps

From zero to your first data.

01

Create an account

curl -X POST -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"email":"you@example.com"}' "{{BASE}}/api/account"

The response contains the api_key and 100 free credits.

02

Use the key

Send the api_key in the x-api-key header.

03

Ask about a company

curl "{{BASE}}/api/dane?nip=7791011327" \
  -H "x-api-key: $KEY"

You get the full report. One credit.
